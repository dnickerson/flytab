Altitude Optimizer — Which Altitudes to Compare?

FD winds data is available at fixed levels: 3000, 6000, 9000, 12000, 18000, 24000 ft. How should we present them?

A

FD Reporting Levels Only

Show exactly the altitudes the wind data covers: 3,000 · 6,000 · 9,000 · 12,000 · 18,000

Winds are already interpolated to nearest level, so these are the most accurate. Simple and honest about data resolution.

5 rows · directly matches wind data

B

VFR Hemispheric Rule Altitudes

Odd+500 eastbound, even+500 westbound, auto-picked from route bearing:
3,500 · 5,500 · 7,500 · 9,500 (east) or 4,500 · 6,500 · 8,500 (west)

Reminds pilot of legal VFR altitudes. But winds interpolated from nearest FD level — less precise at intermediate altitudes.

3–4 rows · route-direction aware

C

Both — IFR Thousands + VFR +500

Show IFR altitudes (odd/even thousands by direction) plus the VFR +500 variants side by side:
3,000 / 3,500 · 6,000 / 6,500 · 9,000 / 9,500 · 12,000

Complete picture but more rows. Useful for IFR-rated pilot flying either ruleset.

6–8 rows · most complete

Note: RV-9A O-360 practical ceiling ~12,000–14,000 ft. Rows above 14,000 ft would be grayed out regardless of choice.
